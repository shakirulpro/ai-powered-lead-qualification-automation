# 🚀 AI-Powered Lead Qualification Automation

An intelligent lead qualification workflow built with **n8n, AI, Google Sheets, and Slack** to automate lead analysis, scoring, and team notifications.

## 📌 Project Overview

Managing incoming leads manually can be time-consuming and inefficient. This project automates the lead qualification process by collecting lead information, analyzing lead intent using AI, assigning qualification scores, and forwarding qualified leads to Google Sheets while notifying the team through Slack.

The goal is to help businesses streamline lead management, reduce repetitive tasks, and focus on high-potential prospects.

## ⚙️ Workflow Architecture

1. **Lead Collection** — Collects lead information submitted through a website form.
2. **AI-Powered Lead Analysis** — Analyzes the submitted information and classifies lead intent.
3. **Lead Scoring** — Assigns a score based on the lead's qualification criteria.
4. **Qualification Decision** — Determines whether a lead meets the qualification threshold.
5. **Google Sheets Integration** — Stores qualified lead information for tracking and follow-up.
6. **Slack Notifications** — Notifies the team when a qualified lead is identified.
